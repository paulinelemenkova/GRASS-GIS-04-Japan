# GRASS GIS and GDAL Scripts — Marine Geophysical and Topographical Analysis of the Japanese Archipelago

GRASS GIS and GDAL shell scripts used to produce the figures in the peer-reviewed article by Polina Lemenkova. The scripts perform topographical and geophysical analysis of the Japanese Archipelago from NOAA marine geophysical datasets and a GEBCO bathymetric grid.

**Published in:** *Geomatics and Environmental Engineering* **2020**, *14*(4), 25–45
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.7494/geom.2020.14.4.25
**Journal (open access):** https://journals.bg.agh.edu.pl/GEOMATICS/2020.14.4/geom.2020.14.4.25.pdf
**figshare:** https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.13251881
**Zenodo:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.4279236
**HAL:** https://hal.science/hal-03011892
**SSRN:** https://ssrn.com/abstract=3732734

## Contents
GRASS GIS and GDAL shell scripts mapping the bathymetry/topography (GEBCO), geoid undulations (EGM96), marine free-air gravity anomaly, magnetic anomaly (EMAG2), sediment thickness (GlobSed) and terrain derivatives (r.slope.aspect for slope and aspect, shaded relief) of the Japanese Archipelago, with cartographic display via r.colors, r.contour, d.rast, d.legend and d.text.
